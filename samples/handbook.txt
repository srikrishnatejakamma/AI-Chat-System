Northstar Studio — Team Handbook

Working hours
The studio's core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM, Monday through Friday.
Team members can choose their remaining hours around these times.

Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Remote days should be
recorded in the team calendar by Friday for the following week.

Learning budget
Each employee has an annual learning budget of INR 30,000. Courses, books, and
conference tickets are eligible. Ask your manager for approval before purchasing.

Equipment support
Contact the IT service desk at support@northstar.example for equipment issues.
The service desk responds within one business day.

Leave requests
Submit planned leave at least five business days in advance. The annual paid
leave allowance is 20 days. Unused leave of up to five days can carry forward.
